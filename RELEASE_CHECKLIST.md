# Release checklist

The project is a local application. No public website or deployment is
configured. Do not announce or publish a website until every applicable gate
below is complete and reviewed.

## Website launch gates

- [ ] Select and connect the custom domain.
- [ ] Add and verify the final website favicon on the deployed site.
- [ ] Publish the reviewed privacy policy and terms pages.
- [ ] Replace every owner, contact, and jurisdiction placeholder in
  `website/privacy-policy.html` and `website/terms.html` with verified details.
- [ ] Select the software license and confirm that the site wording matches it.
- [ ] Verify all public claims and displayed metrics against real project data.
- [ ] Confirm the published pages have no fabricated reviews, user counts, or
  performance figures.
- [ ] Review layout, keyboard access, and mobile behavior on the real domain.

## Visual and copy constraints

- No purple gradients or pill-shaped buttons.
- No fabricated reviews, testimonials, customer counts, or metrics.
- No vague promotional hero text.
- No AI-generated images or marketing copy.
- No custom cursor effects or excessive scroll animation.
- No "made with AI" badge.
- Do not use em dashes.
